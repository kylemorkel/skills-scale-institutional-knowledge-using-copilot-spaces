# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository centralizes the project management processes used by OctoAcme, including initiation, planning, execution, risk management, release, retrospective, and role guidance. The goal is to give teams a shared, lightweight operating model for delivering work effectively and consistently.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme follows a practical, cross-functional project lifecycle. During initiation, the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success metrics, and decides whether to proceed with planning. In planning, the team turns the approved idea into an actionable backlog with scope, milestones, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Execution focuses on delivery in small, testable increments with clear ownership, daily coordination, and visible progress tracking. Risk and communication management ensure issues, blockers, and stakeholder updates are surfaced early and escalated appropriately. Release and deployment are standardized to reduce operational risk and verify quality before production. Finally, retrospectives and continuous improvement capture learning and turn it into actionable improvements for future work.
